NiXel is special free software operating within The Internet of Labour ecosystem/platform (Internet of Labour — abbreviated as iol). NiXeL allows users to register in iol and visually interact with iol capabilities. NiXeL does not have its own servers (back-end) and uses numerous third-party libraries, modules, frameworks, etc. NiXeL is a Graphical User Interface (GUI) providing electronic document management, accounting for users and iol-apps using iol Smart Orders, Smart Contracts, Smart Accounts, and Smart Receipts.
NiXeL assumes the following functions:
- registration of users in iol;
- authorization of users in iol-apps;
- notifications about orders, job statuses, messages, balance status, etc.;
- control of time calculations;
- control of distance calculations;
- payments for work completed by users in iol-apps;
- quality control of contractors (workers) and work orderers in iol-apps — the iol-quality mechanism;
NiXeL has an internal balance of funds, which provides the following capabilities:
- mutual settlements between users;
- currency exchange;
- top-up of balance in NiXeL;
- withdrawal of funds to personal bank details of users.
NiXeL does not provide financial services, but uses the capabilities of iol and licensed Financial Service Providers (FSP).
Also, iol has the capability to conduct Peer to Peer (P2P) transactions directly between users without the involvement of FSPs based on iol Smart Accounts.
To ensure all capabilities of NiXeL, the following app permissions are required:
- notifications (push notification);
- playing ringtones — to play your ringtones for different notification types;
- access to camera and photos — to upload profile photos and read QR codes;
- location (including when the app is off or in background mode) — to calculate time and distance during order execution and the location of the orderer/contractor 1 hour before the start of work. For transaction log recording). Also, this saves your device resources much more than keeping the application open with an active screen.
The wide range of NiXeL capabilities saves users time, as there is no need to register in each iol-app. Logging into them is performed through NiXeL. It also: - reduces the size of iol-apps, as repetitive functionalities are performed in NiXeL; - provides users with security when using iol-apps, as there is no need to grant permissions to every iol-app of interest to the user; - ensures reliability of labor payment, safety of money and assets; - enables participation in commission shares of any iol-apps from one single place.

The NiXeL system is not a payment system, as there is no transfer of funds from user to user. The movement of funds between user balances is carried out based on Smart Accounts, which can be issued automatically by iol Smart Contracts or manually by NiXeL users. In this context, NiXeL plays the role of an accounting system.

iol (The Internet of Labour) is a decentralized/distributed ecosystem/platform as one option for solving the global problem of unemployment. iol is a socio-economic ecosystem/platform. As a distributed system, iol utilizes third-party resources such as GitHub, file and data storage services, cloud services, blockchain, and FSP APIs. iol-app — software operating within the iol ecosystem using iol capabilities.
The basic concept of iol-apps is: the contractor (worker) must arrive at the workplace with tools and perform work for the customer. An iol-app can work with one of the algorithms provided by iol:
- fixed work payment amount;
- work payment calculated by working time (price per hour);
- work payment calculated by distance (price per meter);
- work payment calculated by time and distance (price per hour + price per meter).
An iol-app can be a commercial application and configured to charge users a commission from work payments. In return for commissions paid in iol-apps, iol generates SINHRO in an amount proportional to the commission size and credits it to the user's balance in NiXeL. Thus, SINHRO appears only when real work is performed. Commission is distributed among users in proportion to their SINHRO invested in the given iol-app. Users can deposit and withdraw their SINHRO into/from iol-apps in the P/PS (Project or Profit Stake) section of the balance in NiXeL. SINHRO allows users to receive passive income from commission distribution, which, for example, enables workers with seasonal orders to earn income from commissions of other iol-apps where other types of work are provided.

iol-quality — an organizational and technical mechanism for quality control of work contractors and orderers. iol-quality includes evaluation of work by the orderer, evaluation of the contractor by the orderer, and reviews.
Upon completion of work, the orderer and contractor are prompted to evaluate each other on an 11-point scale: ratings from -5 (very poor) to 5 (very good), 0 - indifferent/neutral. Points for work or order are calculated as the rating multiplied by the amount of SINHRO received in return for the commission. These points are added to the total points of the user as an orderer or contractor in the iol-app where the order was received.
The rating is calculated relative to the maximum points among all users as a contractor or orderer in the given iol-app. Ratings are calculated separately for orderers and contractors.
These ratings are visible to all users in the user profile. The orderer sees a list of potential workers willing to accept the order, sorted in descending order of rating.
After rating (or opting out of rating), the worker and orderer are prompted to leave a review. For this purpose, NiXeL opens a group chat for the "iol-app + user" pair. In this chat, they can resolve disputes or thank each other. Other iol users can read this chat. Any user can open all group chats of a user from that user's profile.
Upon receiving an order offer by a potential contractor or a notification by an orderer about a potential worker, users can open each other's profiles to view ratings and reviews. This helps them make a final hiring decision.

IOU — "I Owe You" of the Internet of Labour iol-ecosystem/platform: the idea of IOU is that any work performed in one location should cost the same in any other location. For example, if you performed work in one country for 1 IOU, it means you can order the same work anywhere else for 1 IOU. However, a problem arises: work in different countries has different costs. To solve this issue, iol proposes that 1 IOU should equal the minimum wage per hour of work in the country where the work is performed.
The iol architecture allows workers and orderers to settle payments for labor using IOU. Simultaneously, this can solve local and global problems associated with the traditional economy.
In essence, IOU is not any currency or asset. IOU is considered an "Unnamed bearer promissory note". This ensures mutual debt settlement.
IOU cannot be bought or sold for any currency. Thus, a "work-price" balance is achieved in countries with different economic levels, aligning with local regulations.

SINHRO — an internal mechanism for synchronizing the balance in iol, official national currencies, regulated assets, commission shares of iol-apps, copyright and intellectual rights, digital assets, global intellectual, security, and financial structures, as well as old and new economies. SINHRO can be used in decentralized governance of iol.
SINHRO is backed solely by real labor.
Procedure for issuing SINHRO:
- upon completion of work received in any iol-app, in exchange for deducted commissions, iol calculates the amount of SINHRO at the current exchange rate to the currency of labor payment. This amount of SINHRO is credited to the balance of the user who paid the commission;
- for authors, when creating and improving components, iol issues SINHRO and credits it to their balance.
The exchange rate of SINHRO relative to other currencies and assets is determined by transactions executed between users. Users wishing to exchange between SINHRO and another currency can do so by tapping SINHRO in the NiXeL balance:
- view buy/sell offers for SINHRO from other users waiting for matching offers. Selecting an offer from this list and specifying the amount will cause the transaction to be executed immediately by SinhroBot;
- place a buy/sell order at the desired price and quantity. If there are matching offers in the system, SinhroBot will execute the deal immediately; otherwise, it will be visible in the "Others' Offers" list to other users. Unfulfilled personal offers appear in the "My Offers" list. The amount of offered asset will be moved to the Pending field of the balance. In "My Offers", one can cancel their offers.
The current exchange rate of SINHRO relative to a currency is determined by the last executed transaction.
If there is no current exchange rate relative to a currency yet, the rate is calculated relative to an existing SINHRO rate with conversion using FSP currency exchange rates. To limit the amount of SINHRO in circulation, the following SINHRO destruction/redemption mechanism is applied:
- when performing work in exchange for SINHRO ("payment in SINHRO"), the iol-app commission is charged in the same amount as when paying in other currencies. However, in this case it makes no sense to issue SINHRO in return for the commission. Therefore, such commission is distributed to no one and remains in the iol-app account as a rating parameter of that iol-app forever. Since an iol-app is not an individual and cannot manage its balance, this amount of SINHRO no longer participates in circulation and total supply. This counteracts SINHRO inflation, thereby preventing its devaluation.
SinhroBot can prevent exchange rate manipulation. For example, if an attacker wants to manipulate the current SINHRO price by attempting to sell a small volume of SINHRO after placing a buy order below what others offer, SinhroBot will execute it as follows: SinhroBot will buy from them at their price and sell at the highest price among interested buyers. Thus, this will only lock in a higher price for SINHRO, and given that SINHRO is backed by real work performed across all iol-apps, SINHRO will lean toward stable growth.
SINHRO gives any user the opportunity to participate in commission shares of all iol-apps from a single place.
SINHRO is not any currency or asset in countries where taxes are not accepted in the form of SINHRO and cannot be subject to regulation under the laws of such countries.
ATTENTION! Beware of scammers! SINHRO is not sold or exchanged anywhere except in the official NiXeL application.

Insurance — an iol feature designed for full or partial coverage of unforeseen expenses incurred while executing work obtained through iol-apps.
Providing traditional insurance services requires in-depth mathematical analysis of statistics. Currently, iol does not possess sufficient statistics to offer fixed insurance premiums. Therefore, this functionality operates on principles of social responsibility, meaning payments are voluntary and non-fixed. That is, users decide for themselves which insurance case to contribute to and what amount.
For example, in an iol-app for painters, a user received an order to paint a ceiling. However, the orderer's dog accidentally spilled paint on the carpet, and the orderer did not see how it happened. The worker or orderer can agree on who will file an insurance claim in NiXeL. One of them can open a claim in NiXeL. Other users can view the history of similar claims in the profiles of the claimant and contractor and decide whether to cover part of the requested amount.
The procedure for this feature is set forth in the subsections of the "Terms of Use" section of this Smart Contract T0.

2. Procedure for Work Calculations

2.1. When executing a transaction between orderer and contractor (the parties) in any iol-app, i.e., hiring a contractor, NiXeL calculates the preliminary amount (=Psum) of work based on the iol-app settings and shifts the order smart contract to the "Accepted by parties" state.

2.2. If cash payment was not selected in the transaction, the preliminary amount in the orderer's balance shifts to "Pending".

2.3. The parties receive a notification in NiXeL about expected execution of the work order and are prompted to share their real-time location. The notification is sent before the start of work at a time defined in the iol-app settings. This assists in a quick meeting of the parties at the workplace.

2.4. Either party can notify the system of the start of work by pressing the corresponding button. The system then sends the other party a start-of-work notification and begins tracking according to the iol-app settings.

2.5. The contractor can notify the system of a pause (temporary suspension) of work by pressing the corresponding button. The system then sends the orderer a notification thereof and pauses payment tracking.

2.6. Either party can notify the system of work completion by pressing the corresponding button. Upon doing so, the system performs the following:
- sends the other party a completion notification;
- calculates the final actual amount (=Fsum) according to the iol-app settings;
- if Psum equals Fsum, the Psum amount is deducted from the orderer's "Pending" balance and credited to the contractor's balance;
- if Psum is greater than Fsum, the Fsum amount is deducted from the orderer's "Pending" balance and credited to the contractor's balance. Psum-Fsum from the orderer's "Pending" balance is returned to "Available";
- if Psum is less than Fsum, the Fsum amount is deducted from the orderer's "Pending" balance and credited to the contractor's balance. Fsum-Psum is deducted from the orderer's balance and credited to the contractor's balance. In this case, if Fsum-Psum exceeds what is available on the orderer's balance, only the available amount is deducted, the available balance becomes zero, and the orderer incurs a debt to the contractor linked to this smart contract.

2.7. Commissions
2.7.1. Upon final settlement for work, a commission may be charged from the parties if provided for in the settings of the iol-app where the order was placed.
2.7.2. Commission rates are specified in the smart contract and deducted from Fsum after settlement with the contractor.
2.7.3. 80% of commissions for work not paid via exchange for SINHRO and IOU are credited to user balances in proportion to the amount invested in that iol-app. 20% is credited to the iol-provider's balance.
2.7.3. In return for deducted commissions, iol generates SINHRO in an amount equal to the deducted commissions at the current SINHRO exchange rate of that payment currency. If this currency has no rate relative to SINHRO, a cross-rate calculation relative to existing rates is used via FSP currency rates.
2.7.4. The number of SINHRO corresponding to the commission is added to the rating of this iol-app.
2.7.5. If SINHRO or IOU was chosen by the order parties in exchange for work, the number of SINHRO corresponding to the commission is removed from iol and added to the rating of this iol-app.

2.8. Royalty/Donation is deducted from the parties after commission payment.

2.9. Commissions and Royalty are paid in the currency in which work settlement was conducted. If recipients do not have a balance in that currency, the system automatically opens a balance in that currency.
